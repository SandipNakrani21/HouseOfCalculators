Low-value content rejection: code review and fixes

10 October 2026 · Project: D:\Calculator · Scope: page generation, content registries, calculators and tools, supporting guides, localization, ad inventory and quality checks.

What Google actually reported

The supplied AdSense screenshot identifies Low value content. It asks for authentic useful content or tools, ongoing curation and maintenance, and genuine user interest. It does not identify an individual URL or state that a missing legal page caused the rejection.

I read the supplied Publisher Policies text and checked the official site readiness guidance, inventory-value policy and Search spam policies. The issues below are source-code findings relevant to that rejection; they are not a claim that Google disclosed its internal assessment. Translations, shared templates and useful calculators are not automatically prohibited. No minimum word count or article quota is imposed here.

Findings and completed corrections

Priority / findingEvidence and impactImplemented correction
P1 · Tool pages lack supporting examplesAll 23 tool routes previously rendered a single short explanation alongside the interface. For example, Percentage and Working Days did not show fixed cases explaining different outcomes.Added localized accessible example tables and limitations. Examples cover leap days, month-end clamping, ISO year boundaries, birthdays, malformed Roman numerals, percentages, decimal words, savings, debt and budget allocations. Random examples distinguish probabilities from promised results; no public password is suggested for use.
P1 · Calculator explanation panels omit worked inputs and outputsThe 60 model definitions have explanatory paragraphs, but several finance descriptions are brief and lack an explicit static illustration. A formula alone does not show which values produced the answer. Example: Mortgage.Added a server-rendered worked-example block to every calculator page. It lists visible initial inputs, resolved selections, country, model version, computed outputs and result-specific notes. The same model code produces the illustration and interactive result. Category-specific limitations distinguish estimates from official decisions, diagnoses or safety assessments.
P1 · Ad eligibility is too broadThe earlier route-family rule accepted any detail-shaped path, including future or unknown slugs, and the home page. Country models could enter the manual inventory without published source coverage.Replaced the family rule with a generated manifest of 176 known content paths in the 12 available locales. Home pages, navigation listings, legal pages, component demonstrations, unknown paths and country models without explicit source coverage do not request manual slots. The quality check fails if the manifest becomes stale. This is an engineering inventory decision, not Google approval.
P1 · Model confidence and review coverage are unclearSource-review records cover a subset of statutory models. Earlier About wording also implied that every model had a written formula. This can overstate what has actually been checked.Country models without source records now display an explicit limitation and exclude manual ads. About now explains automated checks, independent-review limits and how to report discrepancies using non-sensitive inputs. Review coverage is not expanded by inventing credentials or claiming an authority endorsed the model.
P2 · Financial guides omit relevant referencesThe 12 guide definitions include original explanatory structure and examples, but do not include source links supporting their financial topics.Added relevant official references to 11 financial guides: Investor.gov for compound growth, CFPB for amortization and APR, GOV.UK for the UK VAT example, BLS for US inflation and IRS for US tax brackets. Country scope is explicit. The elementary percentage guide retains its own arithmetic without an unrelated citation.
P2 · Ad precedes supporting explanationThe detail template previously placed the first inline ad directly after the interactive card and before the explanation. A real placement-policy violation was not proven, but this gives the supporting method lower prominence.Moved the first inline ad after the explanation and FAQ. Excluded country models also reclaim the reserved sidebar space, avoiding an empty ad rail.
P2 · New content needs localization and maintenance enforcementEnglish-only teaching content or unchecked inventory changes would recreate the quality gap across localized routes.Added complete new interface and limitation text to the 11 complete dictionaries serving 12 locales. Added inventory freshness checks, all-country example checks and localized tool-reference tests. No incomplete language was made available.

Architecture and structure reviewed

Checked the centralized calculator, tool, converter, chart and guide registries; shared detail template; country and subject routes; localized content discovery; sitemap and metadata construction; consent and advertising boundaries; and existing validation/test coverage. Tools continue to load by group, while the new examples are server content. The small inventory manifest is used by the client without importing all calculation engines.

Existing canonical routes and translated alternatives are retained. Useful pages were not deleted or hidden merely to make the URL count smaller. Country accuracy is not certified simply because a page renders correctly. Existing converter examples, safer shared links, privacy controls and disclosures from the previous fix remain in place.

Recorded validation

Passed: lint, TypeScript, translation coverage, inventory freshness and all 54 tests. Calculator illustrations were checked across 980 supported country/model combinations. The production build completed all 4,558 pages. Offline visible-HTML checks passed for 2,010 calculator pages, 276 tool pages, 132 guide pages with official references and 84 navigation pages. The 1,464 country-model pages without published source coverage show a limitation and have no manual ad wrapper. The build retains a non-fatal metadataBase warning; this is recorded in the log and does not establish an AdSense violation.

Tests check illustrative inputs against the implementation, known numerical answers and boundary cases. They do not independently establish the legal correctness of every country rule, real user interest or Google's assessment of originality. Fresh browser screenshots, real Google ad density and live regional consent behavior were not verified in this run. Generated HTML is checked offline.

Before requesting another AdSense review

  1. Deploy this version; local code changes do not update the public website by themselves.
  2. In AdSense, exclude navigation, legal, demo and unsupported model pages from Auto ads, matching the manual inventory rule. The global AdSense loader remains because it also delivers the published Google consent message; the manifest does not control Auto ads.
  3. Verify the published CMP message, public site and ads.txt accessibility and actual mobile ad layout on the deployment.
  4. Continue actual editorial and source review, especially for country rules. Automated examples are useful supporting material, but do not replace editorial judgment or make simplified country models comprehensive.
  5. After the deployed site and account settings are checked, use the existing Request review control in AdSense.

The code corrections are not a guarantee of approval. Only Google can remove the rejection after reviewing the deployed website. No request for review, deployment or Google account change was performed here. Genuine audience interest cannot be created or certified by changing code.
