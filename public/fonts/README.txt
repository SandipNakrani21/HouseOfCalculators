These WOFF2 files are the unmodified Google Fonts web subsets for Plus Jakarta
Sans and Caveat from the site's successful Next.js production build. They are
bundled to keep deployment independent of Google Fonts network responses.
The filenames retain the hashes from that build. Unicode ranges, weights and
fallback metrics are declared in src/styles/fonts.css.

Upstream projects and SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat
See the two OFL.txt files alongside the fonts.
